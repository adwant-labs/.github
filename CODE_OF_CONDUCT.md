# Code of Conduct

Adwant Labs is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and technically rigorous open-source community.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect, patience, and good faith.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking individuals.
- Welcome questions and differing levels of experience.
- Give actionable feedback and accept constructive review.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and project licenses.
- Disclose conflicts of interest when they affect a contribution or decision.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or publication of private information.
- Sustained disruption, spam, or bad-faith participation.
- Knowingly introducing malicious code, hidden telemetry, leaked secrets, or unsafe instructions.

## Scope

This policy applies in repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, project events, and other spaces where someone represents an Adwant Labs project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers of the affected repository. Do not open a public issue containing personal or sensitive information. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and impartially as practical. Retaliation against a person who makes a good-faith report is not permitted.
